The Strategy

The Content Pyramid — not a theory, a weekly schedule.

The 40/40/20 split wasn't a concept from a deck. It was a live weekly content calendar. Every piece of content got tagged to a tier before it was scripted. The logic: 80% of content gives the audience value — useful or entertaining. Only 20% asks them for anything. When trust is high enough, that 20% converts far better than any hard sell.

40%

Education — Build Trust

Social commentary, life insights, opinion pieces. Content that positions the account as having depth — not just entertainment value. "This account makes me think."

40%

Entertainment — Build Reach

Street experiments, comedic skits, emotional stories. The algorithm reward tier — this is what gets shared and drives new followers. "This account makes me feel."

20%

Sales — Convert Trust

Brand integrations, app promotions, product mentions. Kept deliberately low — Malaysian TikTok users unfollow fast when content feels too commercial.

The Process

A production line, not a creative sprint.

One person running strategy, scripting, directing, editing, and analytics can't operate on inspiration alone. The fix was a batch workflow — everything planned and shot in bulk, so the account never ran dry and momentum never broke.

📝

Script Day — Plan the whole week at once

Write 5–7 scripts in one session. Review last week's data first: what had the highest completion rate? Highest share rate? Those findings shape the next batch's themes and hooks.

🎬

Shoot Day — 4 to 8 clips, one day

All filming compressed into a single session. Setup locked in, on-camera roles briefed, scenes directed. A full week of content produced without context-switching to other work.

✂️

Edit — 2 to 5 days, batch mode

CapCut batch rough-cut → select best takes → fine edit → sound design, captions, thumbnail pull. Prepped for scheduling, not published live — removes daily production pressure.

📅

Schedule & Monitor — First 30 minutes matter most

Queue at peak engagement windows. Stay active in comments for the first 30 minutes after posting — the algorithm weights early interaction signals heavily.

📊

Data Review — Every 2 weeks

Not likes. Completion rate and share rate — the two metrics TikTok's algorithm actually weights. Anything with 3 consecutive underperforming posts gets cut immediately.

What I Learned

The real takeaways.

Not for a deck. For anyone starting from zero in a market that punishes inauthenticity.

01

Share rate and completion rate > likes.

TikTok's algorithm weighs these two signals far more than like counts. Build content to be finished and shared — not just tapped. A video with 50K views and a 60% completion rate will outperform one with 500K views and 10%.

02

Virality rewards consistency — it doesn't replace it.

The 300万 video didn't come from nowhere. It came after 3+ months of consistent posting. The algorithm had data on the account. When the right content hit, the infrastructure was there to amplify it.

03

Emotional judgement is the most shareable content type.

Viewers share content that makes them think "what would I do?" — not just content that entertains. The goodness test series worked because it triggered an internal response, not just a reaction. Build for that.
