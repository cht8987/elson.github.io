The Setup

A market window. A bold bet. A race against time.

During COVID-19, I was working with my father's factory on mask wholesale — selling other brands. I learned the supply chain, the buyers, the pricing, the margins. By 2021, I understood this market better than most people. When a business contact invited me to co-found a premium mask brand, I said yes immediately.

We established Golden Quality Trading and prepared to launch GOLDEN MASK — a high-end but affordable medical mask brand. The positioning: gold-quality reliability, accessible pricing. Three-layer and four-layer masks at RM20–30 per box.

The Build

3 weeks. Complete brand + e-commerce + agent system. Solo.

There was no time to be slow. The market window was open and competition was already flooding in. I had to build everything — alone — as fast as humanly possible.

Week 1

Brand Identity & Visual System

Designed the GOLDEN MASK logo, packaging, and complete visual identity. Gold colour palette + premium aesthetic that stood out in a sea of generic medical masks.

Week 2

WordPress E-commerce + Agent Portal

Built the entire site from scratch — product pages, checkout flow, and a custom multi-tier agent system. Agents could sign up, track their tier, place orders, and view profit margins automatically.

Week 3

Sales Channel Launch

Opened agent recruitment, set pricing tiers, began outreach. Used my existing contacts from the wholesale days. Word-of-mouth spread fast — people could see the brand looked premium and the numbers made sense.

🏆 Why WordPress Was Smart

Not custom dev — fast, flexible, low cost. The agent tier system was built using existing plugins, customised to auto-calculate profit splits. The whole stack was up before competitors could even brief an agency.

💡 The Pricing Strategy

RM20–30/box hit the sweet spot — high enough to signal quality, low enough to be accessible. Agents could earn meaningful margins at scale, which made recruitment organic.

Brand Photography

Premium masks deserve premium visuals.

Every SKU was shot and designed to match the gold-standard brand promise. Clean product photography + colour variant sheets that made agents proud to share, and customers confident to buy.

The Agent System

50–100 agents. No sales team. Just a well-designed system.

The agent pyramid wasn't just a structure — it was an incentive machine. Each tier unlocked better pricing and higher margins. Agents were motivated to recruit sub-agents. The system grew itself.

Campaign Shooting

Models, locations, and a brand that looked the part.

To compete in a saturated mask market, we invested in professional model shoots — giving the brand a lifestyle identity that went well beyond competitors still using product-only stock photos.

The Turning Point

I built the brand. He stole it.

⚠ What I Discovered

Around month 8–10, I started noticing things didn't add up. Then the full picture emerged.

🕵️
Secret operations: The majority shareholder was manufacturing and selling masks under the GOLDEN MASK brand name — without telling the other partners, without sharing profits.
📊
Accounting black hole: Company accounts showed clear discrepancies. We hired an accountant to audit. The numbers didn't reconcile. Hundreds of thousands in transactions couldn't be explained.
⚖️
4-year lawsuit: We engaged lawyers and sued. The case dragged from 2021 to 2025. In 2025, I made the decision to drop the case — the legal costs and time no longer justified the outcome.

He used the brand I designed, the system I built, the reputation I cultivated — to run a parallel business that never showed up in our shared accounts. The brand was real. The partnership wasn't.

What I Learned

The brand was built right. The partnership was built wrong.

In 3 weeks I had a fully operational brand, e-commerce, and agent system. In 3 months I had RM200K monthly revenue. But the company dissolved because of something no amount of execution skill can protect you from — a partner who operates in bad faith without proper legal frameworks to stop them.

✓ What Worked

Identified and captured a real market window at the right moment
Clear brand positioning: premium feel, accessible price
WordPress agent system — fast, scalable, low-cost
Month 1 revenue RM60–100K exceeded all expectations
Agent incentive design drove organic network growth

✗ What Failed

No binding partner agreement — only verbal commitments
No monthly financial transparency mechanism
All critical systems ran through one person (me)
Missed early signals of partner misconduct
Chose litigation — which is almost always a loss even when you win

"Even if you're the smartest person in the room, 33% ownership with no legal safeguards means someone else controls your work. The brand lives or dies not by your skill — but by whether you can trust the people who hold the keys."
