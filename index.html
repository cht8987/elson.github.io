About

One person. The whole brand stack.

Hi, I'm Elson 子洺. For the last 14 years I've been the person founders call when they have a real product but no idea how to make it feel like a brand — let alone get people watching, sharing, and buying.

I sit somewhere between strategist, creative director, and content producer. I write the positioning, design the identity, then actually shoot, edit, and grow the social accounts. Most agencies hand you a deck. I hand you a brand that's already moving.

Lately I've taken the same systems thinking into software — designing and shipping my own open-source AI products, with AI agents as the dev team. Brands, content, and now products: same craft, bigger canvas.
