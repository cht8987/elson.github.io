How it works

From first message
to living brand.

No long onboarding, no committee approvals. Here's what working together actually looks like.

1

Discovery call (30 min)

We talk through where you are, what you're trying to build, and whether I'm the right fit. No pitch — just an honest conversation. I'll ask the hard questions early.

2

Proposal & scope

Based on the call, I'll send a clear scope document within 2 working days. Fixed scope or retainer — no surprise invoices, no scope creep without mutual agreement.

3

Kickoff & strategy

We lock in the direction before any creative work starts. You'll sign off on positioning, audience definition, and platform strategy before a single pixel is designed.

4

Build, review, refine

I work fast. Most brand deliverables within 2 weeks of kickoff. Content retainers are reviewed monthly. You have a direct line to me — not an account manager.

5

Handoff or ongoing

Project clients get a full handoff package so your team can run it independently. Retainer clients stay with me monthly — most end up long-term partners.

FAQ

Straight answers to the questions
everyone asks first.

Do you work with clients outside Malaysia? +
Yes — the majority of my work is for Malaysian and Singapore-based clients, but I've also worked with brands across Southeast Asia. All communication is remote-friendly. I quote in MYR, SGD, or USD depending on where you are.
What industries do you specialize in? +
F&B, lifestyle, fashion, creator/IP brands, and tech startups. I work particularly well with brands targeting the Malay-speaking market, the Chinese community in Malaysia, and the broader SEA English-speaking audience. If you're targeting multiple segments, even better — that's where my multilingual edge helps.
How many clients do you take on at once? +
I keep a tight roster — usually 2–3 active retainer clients plus 1 project at a time. This isn't a volume game. If I can't give your brand real attention, I won't take it on. That's why it's worth reaching out early if you're thinking Q3–Q4.
What's the difference between you and an agency? +
With an agency, you're paying for overhead, account management, junior staff learning on your budget, and a deck every month. With me, you get the person who actually thinks about your brand every day — I write the strategy, design the identity, and produce the content. No middlemen, no telephone game.
Can I start with just one service and add more later? +
Absolutely. Many clients start with a brand identity project and then move into a content retainer once the brand feels solid. Others start with content and realize they need the identity tightened up. There's no hard upsell — I'll tell you honestly what you actually need.
How quickly can you start? +
Typically 2–4 weeks from signed agreement, depending on current capacity. For urgent launches I sometimes have a faster track available — mention it in your first message and I'll let you know what's possible.
